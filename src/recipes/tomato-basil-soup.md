  - amount: 1/2 tsp
    item: black pepper

  - amount: 1 handful
    item: fresh basil leaves
  - amount: 100 ml
    item: heavy cream (optional)
---

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Arrange the tomatoes, onion, and garlic on a baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
3. Roast for 35–40 minutes, until the tomatoes are soft and lightly charred.
4. Squeeze the roasted garlic out of its skins and transfer everything to a large pot.
5. Add the vegetable stock and bring to a simmer for 10 minutes.
6. Add the basil, then blend with an immersion blender until smooth.
7. Stir in the cream if using, and adjust seasoning to taste.
8. Serve hot, with crusty bread or a grilled cheese sandwich.

## Notes

- For a vegan version, skip the cream or use coconut cream.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
