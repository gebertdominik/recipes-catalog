title: Classic Buttermilk Pancakes
description: Fluffy, golden pancakes made from scratch — a weekend breakfast staple.

servings: 4
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 15
totalTime: 25
ingredients:
  - 250 g all-purpose flour
  - 2 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tsp baking powder
  - 1/2 tsp baking soda
  - 1/2 tsp salt
  - 400 ml buttermilk
  - 2 large eggs
  - 60 g butter, melted
  - Butter or oil, for the pan
---

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter until combined.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir gently until just combined — a few lumps are fine. Do not overmix.
4. Heat a lightly greased skillet or griddle over medium heat.
5. Pour about 60 ml of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set, about 2 minutes.
6. Flip and cook the other side until golden, about 1–2 minutes more.
7. Serve warm with maple syrup, butter, or fresh fruit.

## Notes

- Let the batter rest for 5 minutes before cooking for extra-fluffy pancakes.
- No buttermilk? Stir 1 tbsp lemon juice into 400 ml milk and let it sit for 5 minutes.
