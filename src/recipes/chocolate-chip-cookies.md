  - amount: 1/2 tsp

  - amount: 170 g
    item: butter, melted
  - amount: 200 g
    item: brown sugar
  - amount: 100 g
    item: white sugar
  - amount: "1"
    item: large egg, plus 1 egg yolk
  - amount: 2 tsp
    item: vanilla extract
  - amount: 300 g
    item: chocolate chips
---

1. Preheat the oven to 165°C (325°F) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
3. In a large bowl, whisk the melted butter with both sugars until smooth.
4. Beat in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla until the mixture is light and creamy.
5. Fold in the dry ingredients until just combined, then stir in the chocolate chips.
6. Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the baking sheets, spacing them well apart.
7. Bake for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are golden but the centers still look soft.
8. Let cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

## Notes

- Chilling the dough for 30 minutes gives thicker cookies with deeper flavor.
- Swap half the chocolate chips for chopped nuts or dried cherries.
